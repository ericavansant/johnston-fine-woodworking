Needham Kitchen

Select an image to open the gallery. Scroll or use the arrows to move through the project; zoom controls are available.

A white face-frame inset kitchen with slim-Shaker doors and a compound-curved range hood. Integrated beverage refrigeration, appliance garages, and panelized refrigerator and freezer cabinetry keep the room composed. The rift white oak island features grain-matched slim-Shaker drawer fronts, fluted end panels, and discreet Bocci outlets.
