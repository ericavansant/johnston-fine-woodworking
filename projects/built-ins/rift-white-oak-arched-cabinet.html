Rift White Oak Arched Cabinet

Select an image to open the gallery. Scroll or use the arrows to move through the project; zoom controls are available.

A veneered rift white oak cabinet set within an arched niche, with a shiplap back and concealed lighting in the open upper section. The curved geometry required a different set of cabinetmaking techniques, turning the built-in itself into part of the room’s architecture.
